# Images needed for index2.html

Replace the placeholder divs in `index2.html` with actual images when you have the assets.

**How to add an image:** Replace the placeholder div content with:
```html
<img src="index_files/your-image.jpg" alt="Description">
```

Or for the hero background, uncomment the img tag and comment out the gradient div.

---

## 1. Hero background
**Location:** Top of page, behind the main headline  
**Description:** Urban skyline, cityscape, or person working in a modern space. Dynamic, professional, "on the move" vibe.  
**Format:** Landscape, 1920×1080 min (or 2560×1440 for retina)  
**Mood:** Aspirational, energetic, startup energy

---

## 2. Product / Workpod visual
**Location:** After "What we build" concept section  
**Description:** The actual workpod unit or prototype – clean product shot. White or light background.  
**Format:** Landscape, 1200×500 (or 21:9 ratio)  
**Mood:** Clean, product-focused, professional
